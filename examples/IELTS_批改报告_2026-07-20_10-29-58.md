## 📝 作文题目

> You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.

The bar chart below shows the percentage of people in three different age groups who used the internet for shopping in a European country in 2020.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

📊 图表核心数据还原

## ✍️ 原始作文

The bar chart shows the information about how many percentage of people buy things on the internet in a European country in 2020. They are divided into three age groups.

Overall, young people like shopping on the internet more than old people. The group of 16-30 age has the most biggest percentage, and the group of 51+ has the lowest percentage.

In 2020, the percentage of young people aged 16 to 30 who buy things online is 85%. It is a very big number. Next, the middle age group from 31 to 50 years old is lower. It was 60%. Finally, the old people group who are older than 51, they only have 20% to use the internet for shopping.

In conclusion, shopping online is popular for young people, but for old people it is not very popular. The difference between the highest and lowest is 65%, which is very big.

### 1. 时态错误

- **原文**：buy
- **修改**：bought
- **解析**：图表描述的是2020年的数据，属于过去时间，应使用一般过去时。


### 2. 最高级重复

- **原文**：most biggest
- **修改**：biggest
- **解析**：most 和 biggest 都表示最高级，重复使用造成语法错误。

## 💬 详细反馈

### 👍 优点与强项

- **结构清晰**：文章有开头段、总体趋势段、细节段和结尾段，结构完整。
- **关键数据覆盖**：提到了三个年龄段的具体百分比（85%、60%、20%）以及最大差距（65%）。

### 👎 不足与改进方向

- **时态不一致**：多处使用现在时描述2020年数据，应统一使用过去时。
- **词汇重复且简单**：如 'big'、'popular' 等词反复出现，缺乏数据描述词汇（如 'account for'、'constitute'、'significant'）。
- **概述不够精准**：总体趋势段仅简单比较了年轻人和老年人，未提及中年组的位置，且未使用 'overview' 等标准表达。

### 📐 文章结构分析

文章段落逻辑基本连贯，但连接词使用单一（如 'Next'、'Finally'），缺乏更丰富的过渡手段。结尾段重复了开头内容，未能有效总结主要特征。

---

## 🎯 下一步行动建议

1. 统一时态：练习描述过去数据时，全文使用一般过去时（如 was, were, accounted for）。
2. 丰富词汇：学习并运用数据描述词汇，如 'account for', 'constitute', 'significant', 'minority', 'majority' 等。
3. 提升概述质量：在总体趋势段中，明确提及所有年龄组的相对位置，并使用 'overall', 'it is clear that' 等标准表达。
