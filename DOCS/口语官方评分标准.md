## 一、评分结构

口语按 4 个维度独立评分，最终 Overall 由四维得出：

| 维度 | 中文 | 简称 | 关注什么 |
|------|------|------|---------|
| Fluency and Coherence | 流利性与连贯性 | FC | 语流、重复/自我纠正、犹豫是"想内容"还是"找词找语法"、衔接词与语篇标记 |
| Lexical Resource | 词汇多样性 | LR | 词汇广度、地道/非常见词、习语、collocation、paraphrase（改述） |
| Grammatical Range and Accuracy | 语法多样性及准确性 | GRA | 简单/复杂句型的使用范围与灵活性、错误频率与是否影响理解 |
| Pronunciation | 发音 | P | 发音特征运用范围、可理解度、母语口音影响（AI 只能间接推断） |

> **跨档桥接规则（关键）**：官方对部分中间档（如 P 的 7 / 5、3 的部分维度）不写独立描述，而是用桥接句——
> *"Shows all the positive features of Band X and some, but not all, of the positive features of Band Y."*
> 含义：该档 = **完整满足低档的全部积极表现 + 部分（但非全部）满足高档**。判定时必须同时对照两个相邻档，不能只看单句。

## 二、各维度档位描述（Band 9 → 0）

### FC 流利性与连贯性

| Band | 官方英文（以英文为准） | 中文要点 |
|------|----------------------|---------|
| 9 | Speaks fluently with only rare repetition or self-correction; any hesitation is content-related rather than to find words or grammar. Speaks coherently with fully appropriate cohesive features. Develops topics fully and appropriately. | 极少重复/自我纠正；犹豫只源于思考内容；衔接完全恰当；话题展开充分 |
| 8 | Speaks fluently with only occasional repetition or self-correction; hesitation is usually content-related and only rarely to search for language. Develops topics coherently and appropriately. | 偶有重复/纠正；犹豫基本是内容性的；连贯且恰当展开话题 |
| 7 | Speaks at length without noticeable effort or loss of coherence. May demonstrate language-related hesitation at times, or some repetition and/or self-correction. Uses a range of connectives and discourse markers with some flexibility. | 能不费力地说长、不失连贯；可有时语言性犹豫/重复/纠正；连接词有一定灵活性 |
| 6 | Is willing to speak at length, though may lose coherence at times due to occasional repetition, self-correction or hesitation. Uses a range of connectives and discourse markers but not always appropriately. | 愿意说长；会因重复/纠正/犹豫偶失连贯；连接词用法不一定恰当 |
| 5 | Usually maintains flow of speech but uses repetition, self-correction and/or slow speech to keep going. May over-use certain connectives and discourse markers. Produces simple speech fluently, but more complex communication causes fluency problems. | 靠重复/纠正/放慢维持语流；过度使用某些连接词；简单表达流利、复杂表达出问题 |
| 4 | Cannot respond without noticeable pauses and may speak slowly, with frequent repetition and self-correction. Links basic sentences but with repetitious use of simple connectives and some breakdowns in coherence. | 明显停顿、语速慢、频繁重复纠正；只会用简单连接词连接基本句子 |
| 3 | Speaks with long pauses. Has limited ability to link simple sentences. Gives only simple responses and is frequently unable to convey basic message. | 长时间停顿；连接简单句能力有限；常连基本意思都表达不清 |
| 2 | Pauses lengthily before most words. Little communication possible. Only produces isolated words or memorised utterances. | 词间长时间停顿；几乎无法沟通 |
| 1 | No communication possible. No rateable language. | 无法沟通 / 无可评分语言 |
| 0 | Does not attend. | 缺考 |

### LR 词汇多样性

| Band | 官方英文（以英文为准） | 中文要点 |
|------|----------------------|---------|
| 9 | Uses vocabulary with full flexibility and precision in all topics. Uses idiomatic language naturally and accurately. | 所有话题都自如精确用词；自然准确使用习语 |
| 8 | Uses a wide vocabulary resource readily and flexibly to convey precise meaning. Uses less common and idiomatic vocabulary skilfully, with occasional inaccuracies. Uses paraphrase effectively as required. | 词汇丰富灵活、达意精准；熟练使用非常见词/习语（偶尔欠准）；按需有效改述 |
| 7 | Uses vocabulary resource flexibly to discuss a variety of topics. Uses some less common and idiomatic vocabulary and shows some awareness of style and collocation, with some inappropriate choices. Uses paraphrase effectively. | 灵活讨论多样话题；用一些非常见词/习语，有语体与搭配意识但偶选词不当；有效改述 |
| 6 | Has a wide enough vocabulary to discuss topics at length and make meaning clear in spite of inappropriacies. Generally paraphrases successfully. | 词汇够说长、意思清晰（虽偶使用不当）；基本能成功改述 |
| 5 | Manages to talk about familiar and unfamiliar topics but uses vocabulary with limited flexibility. Attempts to use paraphrase but with mixed success. | 能谈熟/生话题但灵活性有限；尝试改述、时成时败 |
| 4 | Is able to talk about familiar topics but can only convey basic meaning on unfamiliar topics and makes frequent errors in word choice. Rarely attempts paraphrase. | 熟话题能谈，生话题只达基本意且常选词错误；极少改述 |
| 3 | Uses simple vocabulary to convey personal information. Has insufficient vocabulary for less familiar topics. | 只用简单词表达个人信息；生话题词汇不足 |
| 2 | Only produces isolated words or memorised utterances. | 只会零散单词或背诵句 |
| 1 | No communication possible. No rateable language. | 无法沟通 / 无可评分语言 |
| 0 | Does not attend. | 缺考 |

### GRA 语法多样性及准确性

| Band | 官方英文（以英文为准） | 中文要点 |
|------|----------------------|---------|
| 9 | Uses a full range of structures naturally and appropriately. Produces consistently accurate structures apart from 'slips' characteristic of native speaker speech. | 自如得体用全套结构；除母语者式口误外始终准确 |
| 8 | Uses a wide range of structures flexibly. Produces a majority of error-free sentences with only very occasional inappropriacies or basic/non-systematic errors. | 灵活用多种结构；绝大多数句子无错，仅极偶不当/非系统性小错 |
| 7 | Uses a range of complex structures with some flexibility. Frequently produces error-free sentences, though some grammatical mistakes persist. | 较灵活用复杂结构；常出无错句，但错误仍存在 |
| 6 | Uses a mix of simple and complex structures, but with limited flexibility. May make frequent mistakes with complex structures, though these rarely cause comprehension problems. | 简单+复杂混合但灵活性有限；复杂结构常错但极少造成理解障碍 |
| 5 | Produces basic sentence forms with reasonable accuracy. Uses a limited range of more complex structures, but these usually contain errors and may cause some comprehension problems. | 基本句型较准确；复杂结构范围有限且通常有错、会造成理解困难 |
| 4 | Produces basic sentence forms and some correct simple sentences but subordinate structures are rare. Errors are frequent and may lead to misunderstanding. | 基本句型+部分正确简单句，但从句极少；错误频繁且会导致误解 |
| 3 | Attempts basic sentence forms but with limited success, or relies on apparently memorised utterances. Makes numerous errors except in memorised expressions. | 尝试基本句型但成功率低，或依赖背诵句；除背诵内容外错误很多 |
| 2 | Cannot produce basic sentence forms. | 无法产出基本句型 |
| 1 | No communication possible. No rateable language. | 无法沟通 / 无可评分语言 |
| 0 | Does not attend. | 缺考 |

### P 发音

| Band | 官方英文（以英文为准） | 中文要点 |
|------|----------------------|---------|
| 9 | Uses a full range of pronunciation features with precision and subtlety. Sustains flexible use of features throughout. Is effortless to understand. | 精确细腻用全套发音特征；全程灵活维持；听者毫不费力 |
| 8 | Uses a wide range of pronunciation features. Sustains flexible use of features, with only occasional lapses. Is easy to understand throughout; L1 accent has minimal effect on intelligibility. | 用多种发音特征；仅偶有偏差；全程易懂、母语口音影响极小 |
| 7 | Shows all the positive features of Band 6 and some, but not all, of the positive features of Band 8. | 桥接档：B6 全部 + B8 部分（非全部） |
| 6 | Uses a range of pronunciation features with mixed control. Shows some effective use of features but this is not sustained. Can generally be understood throughout, though mispronunciation of individual words or sounds reduces clarity at times. | 发音特征掌握程度不一；不能持续有效；基本可懂但个别词/音发音不准会降低清晰度 |
| 5 | Shows all the positive features of Band 4 and some, but not all, of the positive features of Band 6. | 桥接档：B4 全部 + B6 部分（非全部） |
| 4 | Uses a limited range of pronunciation features. Attempts to control features but lapses are frequent. Mispronunciations are frequent and cause some difficulty for the listener. | 特征范围有限；时有偏差；常发音错、给听者造成**一些**困难 |
| 3 | Shows some of the features of Band 2 and some, but not all, of the positive features of Band 4. | 桥接档：含 B2 部分 + B4 部分积极表现 |
| 2 | Speech is often unintelligible. | 表达通常无法理解 |
| 1 | No communication possible. No rateable language. | 无法沟通 / 无可评分语言 |
| 0 | Does not attend. | 缺考 |

## 三、评分执行算法（给 AI / 前端的使用规则）

1. **逐维定档**：对 FC / LR / GRA / P 各自从 Band 9 向下，找出该回答**完全满足描述**的最高整档。
2. **微调 ±0.5**：结合证据判断是否在整档间浮动（本工具整档内按半档判定）。
3. **举证**：每维必须引用 ≥2 处转写文本/音频元数据中的具体证据。
4. **桥接档判定**：命中 P 的 7 / 5 档时，须同时核对低档全部与高档部分特征，逐项打勾。
5. **AI 局限标注**：P 维只能凭文本 + 音频元数据**间接推断**，评分结论须标注"AI 推断，仅供参考"。

## 四、前端量化硬约束（与档位挂钩）

| 元数据 | 阈值 | 结果 |
|--------|------|------|
| WPM | < 100 | FC ≤ 6.0 |
| 停顿占比 | > 10% | FC ≤ 5.5 |
| 填充词占比 | > 5% | FC 上限 −0.5 |
| 自我纠正 | > 5 次/2 分钟 | 语言控制力不足 |
| 音量变化方差 | < 0.15 / 0.15–0.35 / > 0.35 | P 降档 / 正常 / 语调丰富 |
